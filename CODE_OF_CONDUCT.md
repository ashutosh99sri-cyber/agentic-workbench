# Community conduct

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project-managed spaces. Participants should help others contribute safely and
make technical disagreements useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the code, evidence, and impact of a proposal. Critique ideas without
  attacking the person presenting them.
- Be respectful of differences in experience, background, identity, and ability.
  Explain assumptions and accept corrections.
- Keep contributions relevant, credit others' work, and respect licenses,
  confidentiality, and consent.
- Use synthetic examples and redacted evidence. Protect personal information,
  credentials, private source, and security reports.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, repeated personal attacks, and publishing another person's private
information without consent are unacceptable. Do not use project spaces for spam
or to retaliate against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and response

Report conduct concerns privately to
[ashutosh99.sri@gmail.com](mailto:ashutosh99.sri@gmail.com), the contact explicitly
authorized by the repository owner for this purpose. The responsible maintainer
is [@ashutosh99sri-cyber](https://github.com/ashutosh99sri-cyber).

Include relevant links, a concise description and only the personal information
needed to understand the incident. Keep private reports and sensitive evidence
out of public issues. Suspected vulnerabilities use the separate GitHub private
reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Owner authorization of this
contact does not establish a response-time guarantee or tested mail delivery.

Assigned maintainers should review reports fairly, limit access to sensitive
evidence, and decide an appropriate response based on the conduct and its impact.
Responses may include removing content, requesting a change in behavior,
restricting participation, or escalating through the hosting platform's abuse
process. A maintainer involved in a report should seek an uninvolved reviewer.
No response-time guarantee or absolute confidentiality is promised.
